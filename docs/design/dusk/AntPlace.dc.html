Arco de
Santa Catalina

350 m north · 4 min walk · outdoor · always open

The yellow arch over Calle del Arco, with a clock on top. Stand north of it to frame Volcán de Agua through the arch.

You asked: “Why was it built?”

So the nuns of the Santa Catalina convent could cross the street without being seen. The clock came later.

DUSK · DESIGN SYSTEM v0.1

The sky is the clock.

A night-dark app under a live sky. The sky gradient comes from the time, the on-device sunrise and sunset, and the saved forecast. Everything else stays quiet: one sand accent, glass for controls, and huge place names cut off by their cards.

PlaceCard
16 min walk You said street food. Covered if it rains. Triana Market

Parts: tile · walk pill · rank · reason · name. Walk time and name come from code instantly; the reason streams from Bonsai-2, or code writes one if the model fails. Whole card is a link to the place.

TimeChips + TabBar
Morning Afternoon Rain
Now Map Plan Talk

Time chips are a radiogroup and the spec’s “set the time” control: picking one re-scores and repaints the sky. In the real app it is a scrubber with the same four stops. Tab bar floats 26 px up, glass, mic always last.
